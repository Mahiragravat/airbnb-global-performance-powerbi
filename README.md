# 🏠 Airbnb Global Performance Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Airbnb's global marketplace performance across listings, cities, hosts, property types, pricing, and customer review engagement.

The project focuses on transforming Airbnb data into meaningful business insights through data modeling, DAX calculations, interactive visualizations, and dashboard storytelling.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze Airbnb's global marketplace and understand:

- How Airbnb listings have evolved over time
- Which cities contribute the largest share of listings
- How property types influence pricing
- How host participation varies across the marketplace
- How customers engage with Airbnb through reviews
- How review frequency is distributed among users
- What patterns can be identified from historical marketplace data

The dashboard is designed to provide an executive-level overview while allowing users to explore individual business dimensions interactively.

---

## 📌 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level view of Airbnb's global marketplace.

**Key metrics:**

- Total Listings
- Total Cities
- Total Hosts
- Property Types
- Total Reviews

**Key analysis:**

- Listing growth over time
- Property type contribution
- Marketplace evolution
- Major growth and decline periods

---

### 2. Market Share & Pricing Analysis

This page focuses on geographical distribution and pricing patterns.

**Key analysis:**

- Market share by city
- Superhost vs. non-Superhost listings
- Cumulative market contribution
- Average price by property type

The analysis helps identify major Airbnb markets and understand pricing differences across accommodation types.

---

### 3. Review & Engagement Analysis

This page analyzes customer engagement through Airbnb's review activity.

**Key analysis:**

- Total reviews
- Total reviewers
- Review frequency distribution
- Reviews per reviewer
- Cumulative reviewer percentage
- Monthly review patterns
- Review engagement trends

A Pareto-style analysis was also implemented to understand how review activity is distributed across users.
